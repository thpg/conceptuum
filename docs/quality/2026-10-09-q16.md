## Added meanings and distinctions

The new monetary-amount family contains income, personal and earned income,
personal unearned income, employee pay, fixed-period salary, hourly pay,
gross/net pay, monetary expenditure, household fixed/variable expenses,
monetary cost, and interest charges. Interest rate remains a separate rate
quantity. The dictionary's asset genus for amount, fixed-charge description
of all interest, and tax-deductibility restriction on expenses are not copied.

Budget plans have personal and household specializations. The separate
allocated-budget meaning is a monetary fund. Savings and emergency reserves
are resources, while saving money, spending money, money management and
budgeting are processes. The accounting deferred-revenue sense of unearned
income and the business-profit sense of net income are excluded from the
personal-income and payroll additions.

Selected payroll, rate, and everyday budget terms are checked against the
[CFPB financial-education glossary](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/youth-financial-education/glossary/).
The [budget teaching guide](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_building_block_activities_learning-about-budgets_guide.pdf)
supports the plan meaning. The [emergency-fund explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/)
supports the reserve meaning. No recommended allocation or reserve size is imported.

[Bank of England: What is money?](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/explainers/what-is-money)
supports keeping monetary resources broader than physical banknotes and coins.
Existing legal-property money 1957 and artifact/wallet money 2413 remain
separate pending a fuller review; neither is replaced by the new amount quantity.

## Graph connections and limits

Ten non-taxonomic relations connect resources and payment/spending events to
their amounts, actions to money or budget plans, and budget plans to their
money-management purpose. All new assertions use U1 and unspecified strength.
Definitions are regenerated from reviewed graph relations. Existing home
contexts and relation grammar are unchanged.

No account balances, interest calculations, future transaction facts, tax
eligibility rules, legal tender requirements, or jurisdictional deductions
are encoded. The budget is a plan; its expected entries are not asserted as
completed payments. Loan contracts, financial debt, cash/currency, wider wealth
and economic-good meanings, and general preservation/spending still need
review. Continue through [PLAN](../../PLAN.md).
