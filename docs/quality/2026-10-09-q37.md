## Quantities and properties

Physical scalar and vector quantities receive explicit families under the
existing physical-quantity genus. Reviewed mass, scalar speed and energy
records gain the scalar family; velocity, displacement, acceleration and
force gain the vector family. Their previous genera remain. The
[OpenStax vector introduction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/2-1-scalars-and-vectors)
supports these meanings. The IT Scalar record is unchanged.

Mechanical inertia is a property, applicable to resting and moving bodies.
Its definition refers to net external force in an inertial frame, following
[Newton's first-law discussion](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/5-2-newtons-first-law).
It is not equated with rest or with the scalar mass moment of inertia about
a chosen axis. The latter follows the
[mass-distribution definition](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/10-4-moment-of-inertia-and-rotational-kinetic-energy);
area moments and the full tensor are outside this batch.

[Linear momentum](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/9-1-linear-momentum),
[force impulse](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/9-2-impulse-and-collisions),
[angular momentum](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/11-2-angular-momentum)
and [torque](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/10-6-torque)
are distinct vector quantities. Angular momentum does not inherit linear
momentum, torque does not inherit force, and force impulse is not momentum
at an instant. The dictionary's loose genera are not copied into the graph.
Each quantity gets a separate measurement action with an explicit target.

## Paths and motion

A trajectory is the geometric path of a moving point in a selected frame.
Planar and closed restrictions have an explicit intersection, which also
inherits the existing closed plane curve. Geometric closure does not imply
periodic timing. The [motion-path explanation](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/4-1-displacement-and-velocity-vectors)
supports separating a path from the displacement vector and the process.

Travelled distance is accumulated length, including retraced portions; it
differs from net displacement. Its scalar and length genera, and its
measurement target, follow the
[distance/displacement distinction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/3-1-position-displacement-and-average-velocity).

Pure translation keeps rigid-body orientation unchanged. It can follow
straight or curved paths and need not have constant speed, as shown by the
[NPTEL translation lesson](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/112103109/mod_9/lec1/slides/slide1.htm).
Straight translation, curved translation and uniform straight translation
combine independent restrictions. Circular motion gains the curved-motion
family while preserving its reviewed genus.

[Rolling motion](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/11-1-rolling-motion)
contains rotation and relocation components. It does not become a subtype
of pure translation. Rolling with and without slipping are separate
restrictions; fixed-axis rotation remains distinct. Existing generated
records for moving and rolling an object are qualified as transitive
physical actions and linked to the resulting motion.

## Energy and work

[Kinetic energy](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-2-kinetic-energy)
receives translational and rotational restrictions. Their meanings depend
on the selected frame or axis. Physical work is a signed scalar quantity,
separate from employment and torque, following the
[work definition](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-1-work).
No equation is encoded as an inappropriate genus or synonym relation.
