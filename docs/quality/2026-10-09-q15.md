## Reviewed distinctions

- **Circle and disk:** existing disk 2705 receives a qualified Russian label
  and English disk terms. Its misleading circle term is removed and circle is
  attached to the existing boundary curve 2706. The original Russian labels
  remain. Circumference is retained as a legitimate boundary homonym and also
  appears on the separate length quantity with explicit qualifications.
- **Lines and finite objects:** straight lines, finite segments, and the
  existing ray have distinct identities under the broad line/plane-curve
  families. Segments and rays are not classified as complete infinite lines.
  Chords and radius segments use the finite-segment genus rather than the
  dictionary's straight-line parent.
- **Objects and quantities:** radius segments and radius lengths are separate,
  matching the existing Q12 diameter distinction. Circumference, perimeter,
  and arc length are quantities. Width becomes a length while retaining size
  as an ancestor. A planar figure need not have intrinsic dimension two.
- **Parts and species:** a disk includes its circular boundary, and a circle
  has arc portions. These use component assertions. An arc is not classified
  as a circle, and a radial segment is not made a component of the boundary curve.
- **Angles:** the broad existing angle figure is preserved. Its new plane-angle
  subtype has rays, a vertex point, and a separate plane-angle quantity. Solid
  angle is not folded into the plane-angle unit family.
- **Units and instruments:** angular degree is refined through the new family;
  radians, arcminutes, and arcseconds are added. Time-unit aliases are excluded.
  Angle instruments, drafting protractors, digital angle finders, and vernier
  bevel protractors have measurement purposes. The dictionary radio-direction
  sense of goniometer is not treated as a synonym of the entire family.

The circle/disk distinction is supported by Wolfram's
[Circle](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Circle.html) and
[Disk](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Disk.html) documentation.
Only the circular forms are used; elliptical variants of the software
primitives are not imported as synonyms. The selected disk includes its boundary.

Finite segments, complete lines, and half-lines are checked through
[Line](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Line.html),
[InfiniteLine](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/InfiniteLine.html), and
[Ray](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Ray.html).
[Plane Curve](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/PlaneCurve.html),
[Chord](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Chord.html),
[Radius](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Radius.html), and
[ArcLength](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ArcLength.html)
support the remaining object and length distinctions. Closed-loop meaning
is not inferred merely from an absence of endpoints, nor confused with a
topologically closed subset.

[OpenStax: Angles](https://openstax.org/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e/pages/7-1-angles)
supports the two-ray configuration and its measure. Its shorthand describing
a ray as a directed line segment is not adopted as a finite-segment genus.
Plane angle, radian, angular degree, minute, and second are checked against the
[BIPM SI Brochure, version 4.01](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/41483022/SI-Brochure-9-EN.pdf).
SI's dimensionless treatment of plane angle does not erase the radian unit.

Instrument functions are checked against
[STABILA angle finders](https://www.stabila.com/en/products/details/tech-700-da-digital-angle-finder.html),
the [Russian manufacturer terminology](https://www.stabila.com/ru/produkty/detali/elektronnyj-uglomer-tech-700-da.html),
and [Mitutoyo bevel protractors](https://www.mitutoyo.com.sg/sg-en/product/detail/bevel-protractor).
Model-specific precision, material, and spirit-level features are not promoted
to universal properties of all angle instruments.

## Graph changes and limits

Six old genus edges are retained as rejected review history: 4974 (disk),
4975 (circle), 5158 (diameter segment), 5343 (ray), 5934 (width), and 30739
(angular degree). Their former parents remain ancestors through the new
intermediate families. All new relations use U1 and unspecified strength.

Fifteen attribute/component links connect the reviewed figures to their
quantities or parts. Six measurement processes have explicit targets, and
four instrument classes have measurement-purpose links. Definitions are
regenerated from these relations; the relation grammar is unchanged.

The graph does not enforce two endpoints or two angle sides, calculate
lengths or angle conversions, or represent arbitrary geometric instances.
Definition-level restrictions and the proper-arc scope remain review metadata.
The broad point, plane, boundary, diagonal and perpendicular hierarchies,
polygon boundary/filled-area conventions, and solid angle remain pending.
Continue through the [active filling queue](../../PLAN.md).
