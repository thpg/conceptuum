## Corrected structure

Darkness 3549 now belongs to an environmental lighting state, instead of
being a kind of visible light 3449. The latter's label is qualified while
its natural-phenomenon ancestry is retained. The old state/radiation contrary
is replaced by a dark/lit-state contrary. The unqualified light/shadow
contrary is rejected: a shadow may still contain ambient light. Shadow's
existing phenomenon genus and light dependency remain.

The U1 scan falls from nine to **8 conflicting pairs**, without a new
pair or shared witness. This scan covers accepted nonzero genus paths and
exclusion codes 61, 63 and 64; it does not establish complete semantic correctness.

Bright and dim are classified through perceived brightness; light-coloured
and dark-coloured through colour lightness. Neither perceptual attribute
is identified with a photometric luminance value. Existing contrary pairs
are retained. The darkness noun previously attached to a dark-colour
adjective moves to darkness. The malformed comparative `яркее` is removed
from both language tags; the established base adjective remains.

Six pale colours move from the lighting process to their hue families and
also to the light-coloured value: brown, green, pale blue, pink, beige and
yellow. Their Russian terms mislabeled as English are replaced with actual
English labels. The Russian basic blue record receives `light blue`; the
paler variant retains its narrower sense. No numerical colour boundaries
are invented. Q20's earlier dark-grey correction is preserved.

## Added coverage

Photometric quantities are separated from units, measurement activities,
instruments and visual impressions. Luminous flux, luminous intensity,
illuminance and luminance have quantity genera. Lumen, lux and candela per
square metre have unit genera; the existing candela and luminous-intensity
unit family are reused. Each specific measurement targets its corresponding
quantity, and each meter has its measurement purpose.

Daylight, sunlight and moonlight select visible-light meanings. Daylight is
distinct from daytime, sunlight from the Sun object and the wider solar
radiation spectrum, and moonlight from the Moon object. No primary-emission
claim is added for the Moon.

## Sources and dictionary corrections

The pinned [Open English WordNet 2025 base edition](https://en-word.net/downloads)
and [Q11 attribution and reuse notices](2026-10-09-q11.md#sources-and-reuse)
remain in use. Archive SHA-256:
`9ca6d1dcb75f822fdd66617f7d9da48142ace38dd544d6ad5e2feca1674ad3fe`.

The dictionary's luminance/brightness synset mixes physical and perceptual
meanings. CIE's [brightness](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-22-059) and
[lightness](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-22-063) entries support the separate
perceptual meanings. CIE's [luminance](https://www.cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-21-050)
and [illuminance](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-21-060) definitions correct
the dictionary's illuminance-under-luminance hierarchy.

CIE's [luminous flux](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-21-039) and
[luminous intensity](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-21-045) entries support
distinct quantities. The dictionary's luminous-flux-unit gloss incorrectly
adds a per-area restriction. The [BIPM candela and derived-unit guide](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/41489685/SI-App2-candela.pdf)
supports the unit families and the distinction between photometric and
radiometric values. The [CIE meter characterization publication](https://www.cie.co.at/publications/characterization-performance-illuminance-meters-and-luminance-meters)
supports separate illuminance and luminance meters.
Gramota's [bright entry](https://gramota.ru/meta/yarkiy) supplies the standard
Russian comparative form used to reject the malformed alias.

Against Q26, 15,602 synsets have normalized English candidates and
91,917 do not; candidates reach 4,179 existing records. These are
spelling candidates, not verified identities or missing-concept counts.
Every new label was checked across both languages; no exact collision was
accepted. A substring match between `lux` and `luxurious` was not treated as
identity. Source definitions are paraphrased; labels and graph mappings are
reviewed adaptations.
