## Corrected structure and vocabulary

House 149 now explicitly denotes a residential building. It has both
building and dwelling genera. Existing residential premises 4697 is
reused and qualified; apartment 2366 now belongs there. Attic 2375 and
basement 2376 belong to the existing building-component family 24411.
They are not kinds of whole houses.

The selected whole-house/apartment-unit exclusion 8688 is preserved.
Correcting the apartment genus removes its shared witness. The U1 scan
falls from 14 to **13 conflicting candidate pairs**, with no new
pair or witness. The scan covers accepted, nonzero U1 genus paths and
codes 61, 63 and 64, not all possible semantic contradictions.

Six reviewed component links pointed from part to whole. The apartment/room
link now points from apartment to room. Other reversed links are rejected;
whole-to-part facts are asserted only on appropriate restricted subjects.
Seven blanket feature claims on every house or building are removed.
Not every house has a separate bedroom, living room, corridor, attic or
basement; not every building has stairs or a balcony.

Residing 6401 is reused as the selected state of living in a place. Its
physical-action genus, doubled-vowel Russian artifact and Russian verb
tagged as English are corrected. Residential premises now have residing
as purpose instead of generic biological life. No legal residence status
or eligibility rule is introduced.

## Added coverage and intersections

House forms, apartment layouts and component-defined families are separate.
A two-unit building is not a two-level apartment. One-bedroom apartment
does not mean one total room, and studio apartment does not require a
separate bedroom. A semi-detached house selects one joined house in the
British sense. The broader bungalow/cottage ambiguity remains deferred.

A houseboat has boat and dwelling genera; it is not made a kind of land
building. Houses and apartments with balconies share the relevant dwelling
family while retaining their own distinct genera.

Kitchen/dining room, living room/bedroom, and combined bathroom/toilet
records have both applicable room-use genera. The old bedroom/living-room
and bathroom/toilet exclusions are rejected before these shared subtypes
are added. The sleeping-room purpose already curated in Q9 is preserved.

The enclosed stairwell is a space containing stairs, distinct from the
steps themselves, the central void and a single landing. Its qualified
wording does not assert a fire-safety classification or construction rule.
