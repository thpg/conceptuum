## Structural corrections

Bottle opener 2391 now has an explicitly broad label. Corkscrew 2392 belongs
to this family while retaining its tool ancestry. Their old exclusion is
rejected. A crown-cap opener is a narrower function, and a combined corkscrew
and cap opener has both genera. This intersection is supported by the
[manufacturer's opener guide](https://www.oxo.com/blog/coffee-and-beverages/which-wine-opener-is-right-for-you).

The universal peeling-before-slicing sequence is removed. The physical
cutting genus is added to culinary slicing without replacing its existing
food-preparation genus. The assertion that generic cutting acts on a knife
is rejected; existing knife purpose and capability links preserve the tool
role. The remaining peeling/slicing coordinate relation, generic cutting
dependencies and old quantified material claims are queued separately.

Opening, smearing and cutting lose three generated Russian spellings with
duplicated vowels and three English-tagged Russian infinitives. Correctly
tagged Russian verbs and qualified English terms are added. The attested
alias `чеснокодавилка` is retained: the [normative spelling dictionary](https://gramota.ru/storage/public/normdicts/orfograficheskij_slovar.pdf)
confirms it. Unfamiliar words are checked before being treated as errors.

## New coverage

The batch extends existing bakeware and kitchen-utensil families. Baking
moulds include loaf, muffin, springform and round subtypes. A round springform
mould has both shape and construction genera. No fixed well count, material
or universal round shape is asserted. The [pan guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2023/06/05/your-complete-guide-to-baking-pans)
supports these distinctions; the intersections are editorial graph mappings.

Tools include garlic presses, apple corers, manual can openers, kitchen-knife
subtypes, cookie cutters, pizza wheels, pastry brushes, pastry bags and bench
scrapers. Purposes and food targets connect them to coring, coating, piping,
cutting, slicing, peeling and existing preparation activities.
