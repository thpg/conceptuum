## Reviewed distinctions

- **Representation versus value:** numerals, fractions, and decimal forms are
  written information objects. Rational numbers, quotient values, constants,
  and numerical division operands retain the number hierarchy. Different
  spellings of the same value are not introduced as distinct numerical values.
- **Fraction forms:** common, proper, improper, reduced, and mixed forms have
  separate meanings. A common fraction may represent an integer. Proper and
  improper use absolute magnitude for signed fractions; the improper class
  includes the equality boundary, such as 5/5, omitted by the OEWN gloss.
  Reduced fractions can be either proper or improper.
- **Fraction components:** numerator and denominator are expression roles in
  the general notation, allowing algebraic components. They are distinct from
  numerical dividend/divisor values. Common and least common denominator
  meanings use the numerical-fraction scope. The bar is a notation component.
- **Decimal forms:** finite and eventually repeating representations have a
  shared decimal-notation parent. A repeating decimal is not classified under
  the dictionary's restrictive finite power-of-ten-denominator description.
- **Ratios and percentages:** a numerical ratio is a quotient. A part-to-whole
  ratio and a percentage are separate specializations; percentages can exceed
  100 percent. Percentage notation and its sign are separate from the ratio value.
- **Equations and homonyms:** a written equality is separate from equality as
  a relation. A proportion is an equality of ratios. Programming expression,
  operand, variable, and constant records keep their U3 meanings; mathematical
  symbols and numerical constants have qualified labels.

Fraction and mixed-form meanings use
[OpenStax: Visualize Fractions](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/4-1-visualize-fractions).
The signed proper/improper comparison is supported by the fraction overview in
[York University EECS1710, lecture 13, page 3](https://www.eecs.yorku.ca/course_archive/2014-15/F/1710/rw_pub/page0/files/1710f14_C13_2014_10_21.pptx.pdf).
That lecture's implementation-specific zero-denominator handling is excluded.

Expression and equation distinctions follow
[OpenStax: The Language of Algebra](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/2-1-use-the-language-of-algebra).
General numerator/denominator roles and cancellation of factors use
[Rational Expressions](https://openstax.org/books/intermediate-algebra-2e/pages/7-1-multiply-and-divide-rational-expressions).
The separate number/syntax classification is the graph's modeling decision;
the source synsets' value-based parents are not copied automatically.

[Ratios and Rate](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/5-6-ratios-and-rate),

## Graph connections and limits

Six component assertions connect fraction notation to its numerator,
denominator and bar, percentage notation to its sign, mixed notation to its
fractional part, and written equality to its expressions. Seven process-target
relations connect expression/fraction evaluation, ratio/percentage calculation,
expression transformation, fraction reduction, and common-denominator conversion.

All assertions use U1 and unspecified strength. Information object 265 receives
its established abstract-object parent in U1 while retaining its U3 parent and
home context. The relation grammar is unchanged. Definitions are regenerated
from reviewed graph relations, not manually overwritten.

The graph does not yet encode arithmetic predicates, actual fraction values,
denominator nonzero constraints, conversion coefficients, or equation solving.
Those details are source-reviewed metadata; the component/target links are
executable graph assertions. Container-content and causal relations are not
repurposed to mean numeric denotation or arithmetic evaluation.

Existing fractional record 4824 remains pending: its adjective and number
genus do not establish whether it means a property, a non-integer value, or a
representation. General formula 2658 and logical identity 5024 also retain
their existing meanings. Continue with physical quantities and their measuring
