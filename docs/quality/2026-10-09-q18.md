## Corrections to the upper graph

The kitchen review exposed a false exclusion between tools 30 and dishware
127. Colander 1537, cooking spoon 24769 and measuring spoon 24771 are valid
descendants of both. Edge 8095 is rejected, preserving both useful genera.

A separate pre-application witness check caught a second inherited exclusion:
action 19 versus process 2631, edge 8023. Material mixing 3473 already belongs
to both, as does food mixing 24782. Driving threaded hardware is also both a
deliberate action and a physical process. The exclusion is rejected without
forcing either whole branch beneath the other.

U1 positive genus witnesses identified 22 conflicting exclusion edges before
this batch. After the two reviewed corrections, 20 candidates remain. These
remaining cases are explicitly queued, not automatically repaired: an
inconsistent shared child can reflect a mixed sense or a wrong genus instead
of a wrong exclusion. The local projection and final database checks found
no new conflicting edge or newly introduced witness among the remaining cases.

This check covers accepted, nonzero U1 genus links and exclusion codes
61, 63 and 64. It is not a completeness claim about all contexts or inferred
semantic contradictions. Meaningful multiple inheritance remains available
to the Euler viewer; no arbitrary overlap percentages were added.

The two rejected tool-to-fastener co-hyponym edges are retained as history.
The batch removes six generated Russian forms, five Russian verbs tagged as
English, and one misleading English restriction. It inserts
137 term rows. The broader twisting-off, metalworking and attachment records
remain for separate sense and duplicate review.

New distinctions include physical screw threads versus textile or computing
threads; internal versus external thread features; fastening washers versus
sealing gaskets; and fasteners versus the tools and actions that use them.
The dictionary locknut sense is restricted to a jam nut. Its wing-screw alias
is not imported onto the wing-nut concept.

Blind rivets are a broader family than pull-mandrel rivets: installation
from one side does not imply a single setting mechanism. The new hand tool
and installation action are restricted to pull-mandrel rivets. Rivet nuts,
powered riveting machines and the riveter occupation are separate meanings.
A split lock washer receives no unconditional anti-loosening guarantee.
