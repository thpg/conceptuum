relative properties, with known as its correctly scoped contrary. Fame is
qualified as public recognition. Familiar and unfamiliar are relative
properties rather than bodies of knowledge. These distinctions follow the
[unknown](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/unknown),
[familiar](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/familiar) and
[fame](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/essential-british-english/fame)
senses. Antonymy is interpreted for the same observer, respect and time.

to the equivalent public-fame property 5085. Its complete source record is
archived. The separate glory record 5722 is qualified as honored public

Notoriety supplies the negatively evaluated public-fame subtype. The
[fame distinctions](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/thesaurus/articles/fame)
support keeping public recognition separate from approval.

The confidential adjective receives a sharing-restriction genus and actual
English terms. Its existing IT counterpart remains untouched. Literary work
retains its legal-context parent and receives the same ordinary authorship

Anonymous and pseudonymous attribution are separate restrictions.
Anonymity can coexist with public recognition: the widely known anonymous
work has both genera. Literary authorship provides two further intersections.
[Anonymity](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/anonymity) concerns
the disclosed identity, while [pseudonymity](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/pseudonymous)
concerns an assumed name; a pseudonym does not prove that the real name is unknown.

Public access, content correctness and verification are separate axes.
Public misinformation and publicly available verified information each have
two genera. In the selected dictionary convention,
[misinformation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/misinformation?topic=lies-lying-and-hypocrisy)
includes accidental and deliberate false information; [disinformation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/disinformation?topic=lies-lying-and-hypocrisy)
adds an intention to mislead. Other taxonomies can use a narrower convention.
Unverified is not a synonym for false. A checking process can fail or remain
inconclusive. Confidentiality describes intended sharing restrictions, so
confidential information is not forced beneath actual nonpublic availability.

There are 10 new non-genus relations, including information attributes,
scoped contraries and a checking target. No individuals are classified as
famous or notorious, and no actual confidential content is introduced.
