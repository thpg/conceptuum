## Coverage and useful intersections

Composition and physical state are separate dimensions. Liquid solution is
both a solution and liquid matter; solid solution is both a solution and
solid matter. A state itself is not a material sample. The new solid-state
record also stays separate from hardness and the existing frozen wording.

Saturated aqueous solution has both saturated-solution and aqueous-solution
genera. Unsaturated aqueous solution has the corresponding second pair.
These explicit shared subtypes support Euler intersections without invented
overlap percentages. Saturation depends on a designated solute and conditions,
so no unconditional exclusion is asserted between saturation classes.

Solvent and solute are roles within a solution, not fixed liquid/solid
categories. An aqueous solution is not made a subtype of the broad everyday
water record. Existing element and alloy records are not automatically
reclassified from spelling matches.

Dispersed systems distinguish suspensions, emulsions, aerosols and liquid
foams. They are not all forced under chemical mixture: a difference of
physical state does not by itself establish different chemical substances.
Nor is every aerosol forced into the narrower colloidal-dispersion family.
Photographic emulsion and aerosol dispenser remain separate meanings.
