## Reviewed distinctions

- **Quantities and homonyms:** physical area is distinct from a city square;
  spatial volume from concept extension; physical power from political power;
  force from coercion. Each receives a qualified canonical label.
- **Force:** existing resultant force 3399 is renamed to make that meaning
  explicit, retaining its old term. Edge 5669 to physical quantity is rejected
  and replaced by a more specific force parent; physical quantity remains an ancestor.
- **Density:** mass per volume, liquid mass density, and relative density have
  separate meanings. Relative density is a dimensionless ratio to a specified
  reference; it is not classified as mass per volume or universally tied to water.
- **Power and energy:** electrical energy and electrical power have different
  parents. Watt-hours and kilowatt-hours are energy units; megawatts are power
  units. The ambiguous B.T.U. alias is excluded from kilowatt-hour.
- **Units:** density, speed, and acceleration receive unit families. Prefixed
  units are siblings within their appropriate families, not species of the
  unprefixed unit. Symbols retain their reviewed capitalization; the project's
  case-insensitive lexical lookup is not a scientific unit parser.
- **Measuring instruments:** wattmeters target electrical power, electricity
  meters target electrical energy, and force gauges target force. The OEWN
  dynamometer/ergometer power sense is not mapped to the new force gauge.
- **Liquid apparatus:** hydrometers and liquid pycnometers belong to mass-density
  instruments; a liquid pycnometer works with weighing. Generic pipettes serve
  liquid transfer. Calibrated volumetric and graduated pipettes additionally
  belong to volumetric apparatus and serve volume measurement. Pasteur pipettes
  do not inherit a universal calibrated-volume claim.

Quantity dimensions and SI prefixes are checked against the
[BIPM SI Brochure, ninth edition, version 4.01](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/41483022/SI-Brochure-9-EN.pdf).
The instrument family allows use alone or with other equipment, following
[JCGM VIM 3.1](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/3.1.html).

Density distinctions and the roles of hydrometers and pycnometers use
[Anton Paar's density reference](https://wiki.anton-paar.com/au-en/density-and-density-measurement/).
The graph uses mass per volume; casual source wording about weight is not
adopted as a mass/force identity. Gas pycnometers and optical densitometers
remain separate, deferred meanings.

Power versus energy and watt-hour meanings use
[EIA: Measuring electricity](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/measuring-electricity.php)
and the [EIA electricity glossary](https://www.eia.gov/tools/glossary/index.php?id=Electric).
Instrument purposes are supported by
[Hioki power meters](https://www.hioki.com/global/products/power-meters),
[Schneider energy meters](https://www.se.com/uk/en/product-subcategory/4125-cost-energy-management-meters/),
and [Mark-10 force gauges](https://mark-10.com/products/force-gauges/).
No universal AC power formula or device precision is inferred.

Volume apparatus and transfer tools use BRAND's
[volumetric instruments](https://shop.brand.de/en/volumetric-instruments.html),
[graduated cylinders](https://shop.brand.de/en/volumetric-instruments/graduated-cylinders.html),
and [pipette catalog](https://shop.brand.de/en/general-lab-products/pipettes.html).
Glass-only restrictions are excluded where plastic versions exist. Calibration
temperature, containment/delivery conventions, and operating procedures are
not inferred for all members of a family.

## Graph connections and limits

All additions use U1 and unspecified strength. Existing home contexts and the
relation grammar are preserved. Measurement processes have explicit quantity
targets; instruments have intended-process links. Liquid transfer targets
liquid. Definitions are regenerated from these reviewed relations.

The graph does not yet encode numerical conversion coefficients, density
formulas, reference conditions, calibration certificates, or measurement
uncertainties. Formula and source restrictions are review metadata. Unit
symbols and lexical aliases do not implement calculation or dimensional analysis.

Weight 44, broader energy and heat descendants, power-measuring dynamometers,
and optical densitometry remain pending. Geometry and angle measurement are
next in [PLAN](../../PLAN.md).
