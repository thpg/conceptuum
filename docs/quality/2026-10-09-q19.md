## Added coverage

The calendar system, a date table, a date designation, a dated entry and
the event represented by that entry are separate concepts. Calendar dates
are information objects. A calendar day stays separate from the fixed
24-hour unit and from daytime.

Leap/common-year and February 29 records are explicitly scoped to the
Gregorian calendar. Generic calendar-month and calendar-year records do
not acquire that system's numerical rules. A Julian calendar is not a
Julian date. The dictionary's time-zone gloss and culture-specific weekday
and weekend assumptions are deferred.

Scheduling produces a plan; it does not establish that planned events
occurred. Deadline is a time point, while the existing term record denotes
a period. A day off depends on the relevant person's schedule. Academic
year receives no fixed 365-day or January-to-December assertion.
