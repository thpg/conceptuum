## Roles and taxonomy

The existing predator record now has animal as its genus. Predators are not
necessarily mammals. A separate carnivoran concept denotes membership in the
mammalian order Carnivora. Its taxonomy follows the
[NCBI classification](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?command=show&id=33554&lvl=3&mode=tree)
and the [Idaho Fish and Game catalog](https://idfg.idaho.gov/species/taxa/tree/order/Carnivora).
The nine reviewed former predator children retain their mammal ancestry through
this taxonomic family. The broad bear record receives this family in place of
an unqualified predator genus; its old probabilistic hunting capability remains.
The inherited bear/panda exclusion needs a separate taxonomy review.

Prey, predation, predatory birds and fish, dietary carnivores, omnivores,
carrion and carrion feeding are distinct meanings. The existing Russian
herbivore record is reused and gets real English translations. The
[National Park Service food-web lesson](https://home.nps.gov/teachers/classrooms/to-eat-or-be-eaten.htm)
supports separating feeding categories and relationships. The intersections
of predator with prey, and predator with carrion feeder, are editorial graph
constructions. They express compatible roles without declaring every animal
to occupy them or assigning a particular species to them.

## Activities, participants, and objects

A hunter is a person; the narrower occupational hunter additionally inherits
worker. Human hunting has recreational, commercial and household-food
restrictions. Trap hunting combines the human hunting purpose with an
animal-trapping method. Fishing retains its reviewed physical-action genus
and gains the animal-catching family, without becoming a subtype of hunting.

The physical catching activity reuses the old Russian record with the label
ловля. Successful capture remains separate: a fishing or hunting attempt
does not entail success. The six inherited named-fish targets now belong to
carp, pike, perch, salmon, catfish and crucian-carp fishing concepts. They no
longer propagate from generic fishing to every method and location.

Animal traps, live-capture traps, luring actions and physical bait objects are
separate. The existing mousetrap and artificial fishing lure keep their old
valid genera and gain the appropriate new families. Bait is a material role
that can include natural objects and manufactured lures. Live fishing bait
combines the fishing-use and living-animal restrictions; it does not become
a manufactured device through its bait genus.

Physical pursuit is qualified as a chase and moved from physiology to motion.
Protection, already used for roofs, umbrellas, clothing and animal behavior,
receives the broader action genus. The unsupported hunting/protection
disjointness assertion is rejected. Other protection subtypes remain queued.

and [Q11 reuse notices](2026-10-09-q11.md#sources-and-reuse) remain in use.

The comparison was refreshed against Q35. All new terms passed normalized
collision checks; existing Russian records were reused before adding English
counterparts. Dictionary trapping as horse covering, poaching as cooking,
predation as plundering and metaphorical prey are excluded from this cohort.

The indexed [Gramota entry for ловить](https://gramota.ru/meta/lovit)
supports ловля and distinguishes physical catching from figurative meanings.
The [Russian Language Institute pronunciation dictionary](https://ruslang.ru/sites/default/files/doc/normativnyje_slovari/orfoepicheskij_slovar.pdf)

strings tagged as English are removed. Older nonpreferred canonical aliases
are retained for lookup where their wider lexical status has not been resolved;
this is not a claim that all legacy word forms have been validated.
