## Corrected structure

Family 2102 retains its group genus. Its incorrect social-relation genus
is rejected; existing kinship 81 receives the English term `family relationship`.
Friendship is a social relation but no longer necessarily kinship. A blood
relative is a person, distinct from blood kinship as a relation.

The upper object/relation exclusion 7999 is preserved unchanged. Correcting
family's genus removes its shared witness. The U1 scan falls from 10 to
**9 conflicting pairs**, without a new pair or witness. No exclusion
edge is changed or added. The scan covers accepted, nonzero U1 genus paths
and codes 61, 63 and 64, not every semantic contradiction.

Sixteen existing family roles gain nearer shared genera. Mother and father
belong to parent; son and daughter belong to the existing child-in-family
role, which also allows adult children. Brother/sister, husband/wife,
grandmother/grandfather, grandson/granddaughter, aunt/uncle and niece/nephew
gain their shared role families while retaining relative/person ancestry.

Seven male/female co-role pairs had been mislabeled as mutually implying.
A brother need not have a sister; an aunt need not have an uncle counterpart.
Those assertions are rejected. Grandparent/grandchild, aunt-or-uncle versus
niece-or-nephew, and great-grandparent/great-grandchild receive actual
converse-family links. The existing parent/child converse is preserved.

Generic motherhood is not limited to childbirth, and a person's child need
not be in the child age category. The old mother-produces-age-child assertion
is rejected pending a properly scoped birth-event review. Two temporal links
also confused family roles with age sequences: an infant can already be a
son, and generic parents do not invariably precede a particular grandson.

## Added coverage and scope

Household denotes a domestic living unit with one or more members. Its
collection genus permits a one-person household; the multi-person subtype
also belongs to group. A household consisting entirely of one co-resident
family has both household and family genera. Neither broad family nor broad
household is made a subtype of the other.

Nuclear family selects the two-parent-and-children unit; extended family
includes a wider set of relatives. A single-parent family describes the
unit's composition, not the absence of another biological parent. No family
type receives a value judgment or a legal status.

Full and half siblings are distinguished by shared biological-parent scope.
Maternal and paternal half siblings combine with brother/sister roles to
form multiple-parent classifications. The reference person remains fixed.
No DNA percentages or blanket exclusions between person roles are asserted:
one person may have different relationships to different people.

Great-grandparent and great-grandchild keep their distinct genealogical
degrees. A great-grandchild is not made a grandchild of the same reference
person just because that person is a grandchild of someone else.

## Sources and source corrections

The pinned [Open English WordNet 2025 base edition](https://en-word.net/downloads)
and [Q11 attribution and reuse notices](2026-10-09-q11.md#sources-and-reuse)
remain in use. Archive SHA-256:
`9ca6d1dcb75f822fdd66617f7d9da48142ace38dd544d6ad5e2feca1674ad3fe`.

The source is a candidate generator, not an unquestioned authority.
Cambridge's [nuclear-family entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/nuclear-family?topic=family-relations-in-general)
corrects the pinned dictionary gloss that includes grandparents. Its
[extended-family entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/extended-family)
supports the broader family group. Cambridge's [sibling article](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/thesaurus/articles/sibling),
[half-brother entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/half-brother)
and [stepbrother entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/stepbrother)
distinguish half siblings from step siblings; the latter synonyms in the
pinned half-sibling synsets are excluded.

Collins' [full entry](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/full)
supports the same-parent kinship sense. Gramota's [brother entry](https://gramota.ru/meta/brat-54715)
checks the Russian maternal and paternal half-sibling terminology.
The [ONS families and households methods guide](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/families/methodologies/familiesandhouseholdsqmi)
supports the distinction between families and households, including
one-person and unrelated-person households. Its detailed national census
criteria are not imported as a universal definition.

Against Q25, 15,587 synsets have normalized English candidates and

spelling candidates, not verified identities or counts of missing concepts.
Every new name and alias was checked in both languages; no exact collision
was accepted. Existing roles, groups and relations were reused. Full source
definitions are not copied; translations, composed labels and graph mappings
are editorial adaptations.
