## Corrected meanings

Stopping 1798 denotes a transition ending motion. It now belongs to change,
and its old contrary-to-motion assertion is rejected. Rest 3292 remains a
state and keeps its existing contrary pair with motion. Its label now
specifies the mechanical sense, relative to a reference frame. Stopping has
rest as its result; the assertion does not claim indefinite rest.

The shared-descendant scan falls from eight to **7 U1 exclusion conflicts**,
with no new conflicting pair or witness. It checks accepted nonzero genus
paths against codes 61, 63 and 64, not all possible semantic errors.

Relocation 3254 keeps its motion genus and is distinguished from the new
displacement vector. Scalar speed 3462 remains distinct from vector velocity.
Angular velocity is not rotational frequency, and angular acceleration is
not the existing linear acceleration quantity. Earlier direct genera,
measurement targets and the metre-per-second unit are preserved.

Generic slowdown 2926 becomes a change and loses its universal physiological
classification. Physical decelerating motion is a narrower subtype. Its
scope is decreasing speed; it need not reach a complete stop.

Four generated braking records previously belonged to communication. They
are repaired in place with standard Russian nouns or infinitives, real
English labels and physical braking genera. The labels retain general,
completed-action, slight and brief-action wording. Malformed forms such as
`тормозение`, `притормозение`, `затормозение`, `тормознутие` and
`замедлиение` are removed. Valid verbs are restored with Russian language
tags. Figurative hindrance and physiological inhibition are explicitly
outside the selected physical-braking senses.

## New coverage and intersections

The new quantity and unit families have separate measurement activities.
Purpose and quantity names are not treated as interchangeable dictionary
synonyms. No instrument is assigned a new capability merely from its name.

Uniform circular motion inherits both circular motion and constant scalar
speed. Constant speed does not imply constant vector velocity. Nonuniform
circular motion also belongs to variable-speed motion; uniform rectilinear
motion combines a straight path with constant speed.

Mechanical oscillation reuses the existing physical-oscillation process and
also belongs to motion. The ideal simple harmonic subtype is periodic;
one-dimensional harmonic motion additionally belongs to straight-line
motion. Damped oscillation is restricted to genuinely oscillatory behaviour
and is not asserted to repeat an identical state. The displacement amplitude
is a length quantity, distinct from an angular or electrical amplitude.
