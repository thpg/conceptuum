## Corrected structure and vocabulary

Footwear remains within the existing broad clothing family. Its contradictory
exclusion from clothing is rejected. Jeans now inherit from trousers and
denim clothing; the former jeans/trousers exclusion is also rejected.
The U1 exclusion scan falls from 13 to **12 conflicting pairs**, with
no new pair or shared witness. The scan covers accepted, nonzero U1 genus
paths and codes 61, 63 and 64, not every possible semantic contradiction.

Eight inherited material/component assertions were too broad. Every garment
need not contain fabric, nylon, buttons, a zipper or a hook; every shoe
need not have a heel; every sweater and sock need not be wool. Reviewed
component and material facts now use restricted garment families. No
prevalence, composition percentage or protection rating is invented.

Existing coat, jacket and raincoat records inherit through outerwear.
Raincoat is explicitly qualified to distinguish it from a generic cloak.
Sweater, pajamas and apron gain knitwear, nightwear and protective-clothing
genera. Protection includes the wearer or underlying clothes; it does not
assert certification or effectiveness against a particular hazard.
The existing garment pocket now belongs to clothing components.

Laundry keeps ordinary washing vocabulary. One doubled-vowel Russian
artifact and one Russian infinitive tagged as English are removed. The
valid but wider erasure vocabulary is reserved for a separate sense review.

## Added coverage and intersections

Purpose, form, construction and material are independent classifications.
A hooded rain jacket has both rain-jacket and hooded-jacket genera. A denim
jacket combines jacket form with denim clothing. Sports footwear combines
footwear with clothing intended for sport. Protective gloves and helmets
retain their existing garment genera.

Cardigan is open-front knitwear. It is not forced under the narrower
Russian sweater record, and buttons or zippers are restricted to appropriate
cardigan subtypes. Cuffs, hoods, collars, linings and hooks are physical
components, not whole garments. Denim fabric is distinct from jeans.

need not be rubber; only the rubber subtype receives that material fact.

English coat, sweater, vest and hosiery scopes remain review tasks.
