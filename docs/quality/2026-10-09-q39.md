## Containers, materials, and intended contents

The physical-container family is separate from the existing software
container. It includes the reused vessel, whose old dishware genus was too
narrow. Jars and bottles move under vessel, and the existing bucket gains
that genus without losing its artifact relation. The synonym *pail* reuses
the bucket record. Vessel, bottle and jar retain their original search terms;
qualified labels distinguish container, ship, anatomical and quantity senses.

Jars and bottles no longer have universal glass-material assertions. Glass,
plastic and metal vessels provide independent material families. Glass and
plastic jars, and glass, plastic and metal bottles, combine form and material.
The material refers to the body, without requiring a cap or seal to use the
same substance. [Gramota's jar entry](https://gramota.ru/poisk?dicts%5B%5D=42&mode=slovari&query=%D0%B1%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BA%D0%B0)
and [plastic laboratory jars](https://www.thermofisher.com/fr/en/home/life-science/lab-plasticware-supplies/reusable-plasticware/plastic-jars.html)
support broader material and use ranges. [Metal bottles](https://www.hydroflask.com/shop/bottles-drinkware/bottles/)
also demonstrate why the dictionary's glass-or-plastic bottle wording is
too narrow.

Food storage is now a restricted action with food as its patient. The generic
storage action moves from social action to action, and its universal food
patient is replaced by the restricted relation. Existing tool/product
probabilities remain unchanged and need further review. Containers for food
storage have a purpose, which does not require them to be full.

Food-storage jars and glass food-storage jars express use and material
intersections, supported by [glass jar products](https://www.ballmasonjars.com/products/jars/canning-jars/ball%C2%AE-mason-jars-lids%2C-wide-mouth/SP_1888124.html).
These classifications add no food-contact certification, leakproofness
guarantee, sterilization method or preservation instructions.

Metal packaging cans form a separate family. Food cans, beverage cans,
steel cans and aluminium cans are distinct restrictions. Steel food cans
and aluminium beverage cans combine material and use. [Food-can descriptions](https://www.crowncork.com/aerosol-food-packaging/products/food-cans)
support both steel and aluminium constructions; [beverage-can descriptions](https://crowncork.com/beverage-packaging/products/beverage-cans)
support the beverage intersection. Opening or emptying a can does not change
its type. The English *tin can* term does not create an elemental-tin material
assertion. The existing manual can opener gains a can-opening purpose and
metal-can target while keeping its previous opening purpose.

## Brushes and applicators

A descriptive bristled-tool family connects application brushes with the
existing brush record. It permits natural and synthetic working bristles.
The existing drawing brush receives a qualified label and retains its
drawing purpose. The [Active Dictionary of Russian](https://ruslang.ru/sites/default/files/2024-12/ASRYa-4-2.pdf)
supports distinguishing the tool reading from hand, tassel and plant senses.

Applicators have substance application as their purpose. The pastry brush
joins both applicator and bristled-tool families, while retaining its
kitchen-tool genus. Its food-coating action also gains the broader
substance-application genus. [Silicone pastry brushes](https://www.oxo.com/silicone-1-in-pastry-brush.html)
support the synthetic-bristle scope. A pastry brush does not inherit the
drawing-brush purpose.

Paintbrushes and paint rollers have paint application as their purpose.
Drawing brushes can also use ink, so only the explicit artist-paintbrush
intersection belongs to both drawing-brush and paintbrush families.
Paint rollers are not made bristled tools by definition. A hairbrush acts on
hair and reuses the existing hair-arranging action; it is not automatically
a toothed comb or cleaning brush.

The generated coloring label is replaced by a qualified standard action
phrase. [Gramota's usage guidance](https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/285746)
supports the modern action vocabulary. Covering the surface, applying a
substance and applying paint remain distinct levels; a coating action is
not its resulting material layer.

## Cleaning and removal

The cleaning-tool family contains the existing cleaning brush and broom.
The toothbrush gains the cleaning-brush genus while retaining its old tool
genus and tooth target. New nailbrushes target bodily nails, not metal
fasteners. [Bottle brushes](https://www.oxo.com/bottle-brush.html?___store=oxo)
receive a bottle-cleaning purpose and bottle target. [Dishwashing brushes](https://www.oxo.com/shop/cleaning-organization/kitchen/sponge-brushes.html)
reuse dishwashing and dishware.

A hand-scraper family supplies the missing broader genus for the existing
bench dough scraper. Cleaning scrapers combine construction and cleaning
purpose. [Ice scrapers](https://www.oxo.com/extendable-twister-snow-brush.html)
target ice, and [paint scrapers](https://www.bahco.com/gb_en/paint-scrapers-with-rigid-blade-and-plastic-handle-pb_2165_.html)
target paint. Scraping an object is distinct from the sound of scraping,
the resulting fragments and a deferential bow.

Water-removal squeegees have a separate physical action and water target.
[Silicone-edge squeegees](https://www.oxo.com/shop/cleaning-organization/multi-use-squeegee-with-hook.html)
also show why the dictionary's rubber-only construction is too narrow.
Removing water is not universally dirt removal, evaporation or scraping
solid ice, so no such ancestry is forced.

Sweeping moves from autonomous physical process to physical cleaning action.
The broom's unsupported washing-purpose percentage is replaced by sweeping;
its direct claim to cause cleanliness is rejected. Purpose expresses what
the tool is for without claiming that an unused broom changes a surface.
A dustpan belongs to both physical-container and cleaning-tool families and
supports sweeping. No actual debris contents or mandatory short handle are
asserted.

## Terminology and remaining review

The [Russian spelling dictionary](https://gramota.ru/storage/public/normdicts/orfograficheskij_slovar.pdf)
supports the selected sweeping and scraping nouns. Generated forms such as
`подметаание`, `соскребаание` and `храниение` are removed. Russian infinitives
previously tagged as English are retained or added with Russian tags and
replaced by actual English terms. The generated coloring label is removed
from the selected modern action after renaming.

The near-synonymous scraping-off record still needs an aspect and merge
review. Other painting/dyeing labels also remain queued. The generated
record based on *подметить* means noticing, and must not be merged into
*подметать* solely because their spellings resemble one another. Existing
bottle-content, bucket-material, drawing-dependency and wiping-dependency
percentages remain unchanged; their preservation is not fresh endorsement.

The fixed review cohort contains 30 existing records. Direct non-taxonomic
coverage changes from 23 to
24 records, while records without a Latin-letter English term
drop from 4 to
1. The unresolved scraping-off synonym is retained in that denominator.
All 41 additions have Russian and English terms; 28 have direct non-taxonomic
relations. Other additions express restrictions through their multiple
genera and inherited purposes or materials; no unsupported property is added
to meet a coverage count.
