## Reviewed distinctions

- Food preparation 256 includes unheated work. A new heat-cooking subtype
  groups boiling food, frying, baking and bread toasting. Food mixing,
  stirring, whipping, comminution and flour sifting keep their more general
  physical-action ancestors as well as food-preparation membership.
- Cookware is scoped here to vessels and pans. Bakeware is a narrower family.
  A food turner and a mixing spatula remain tools, not cooking vessels.
- Cast-iron pans, aluminum pans and glass pots have material assertions at
  the named subtype. Seven broad material assertions on dishware, pots,
  frying pans and spoons are rejected. Unverified historical percentages
  are not replaced with new guesses.
- A general spoon is retained. Its eating purpose moves to a new eating-spoon
  subtype; cooking and measuring spoons receive their own purposes.
- Kitchen measuring cups and spoons measure volume. They are tools and
  measuring instruments, not laboratory volumetric ware, numerical units,
  or mass-measuring devices. No universal tablespoon or cup conversion is
  encoded. Fixed-capacity dry cups need not have a graduated scale.
- A cutting board need not be wooden; a mixing bowl need not be used with
  an electric mixer. No material, handle count or motor is copied from a
  dictionary gloss as a universal requirement.
- The existing peeling record 1804 retains its food sense and gets a clear
  label. Its shoe-cleaning assertion moves to a separate action. The broad
  brush-to-peeling assertion is replaced by the purpose of a cleaning-brush
  subtype. Household cleaning retains its original household context.
- The toaster's frying purpose and capability are replaced by bread toasting.
  Toasted bread is a food product; toasting is the process that produces it.
