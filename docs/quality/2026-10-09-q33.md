## Repaired distinctions

The five remaining U1 shared-descendant exclusion candidates came from
entries combining incompatible senses. Their upper exclusions are preserved:

| Upper distinction | Reviewed witnesses | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Property / phenomenon | Cheerful; let out a howl | Emotional property and vocal action have separate genera |
| Phenomenon / relation | Being subject to | Existing relation sense retained |
| Process / state | Sleep; take place | Sleep state split from process; occurrence classified as an event |
| Action / state | Being hidden; contesting; lasting; according honor | Each retained record has an explicitly selected sense |
| Physical / physiological process | Mixed walking/raving record | Attested walking verb and vocal raving separated |

Cheerful and sad are emotional properties rather than moral qualities. Mood
is an emotional state; cheerful and sad moods bear the corresponding
properties. This state/process/property mapping is an editorial choice within
the project's ontology, not a claim that dictionaries all share its categories.

Sleep 205 keeps its physiological-process identity and every existing bearer,
purpose and study relation. Its former state genus is rejected, and a
separate asleep state is added. Wakefulness, sleep onset and dozing reuse
existing records with corrected terminology. Sleepiness does not entail
being asleep. The [Russian Active Dictionary examples](https://sensefreq.ruslang.ru/w/RNC%20(verbs)/%D0%B4%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C)
support the selected light-sleep sense of dozing. Inherited probability claims
about sleep and fatigue remain unchanged and are not newly validated.

Producing a howl belongs beneath the existing vocalization process; its
audible result is a separate sound. The mixed record 19387 is retained for
the attested walking verb брести. A separate qualified record represents
бредить as incoherent vocal speech, without assigning a diagnosis.
[Cambridge's rave entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/rave)
supports uncontrolled speech but is broader than the selected Russian sense.
No exact alignment to every English rave sense is claimed. The manifest
archives the original mixed concept, all terms and both genus edges.

Malformed aliases such as настроиение, бодрствоваание and засыпаание are
removed. Correctly spelled Russian verbs replace English-tagged Cyrillic
forms. The ambiguous generated label бредение is retired from the mixed
record; this does not assert that every historical use of that noun is invalid.

Human sleeping is refined by REM and non-REM phases, with three non-REM
stages following [NHLBI's sleep-stage description](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/stages-of-sleep).
These classes denote sleeping during a phase, not a claim that a phase is
the entire sleep cycle. Slow-wave sleep is narrower than non-REM overall.
No mandatory sequence, duration, animal-wide generalization or claim of
exclusive REM dreaming is encoded.

A short daytime sleep has both duration-restricted and daytime genera.
The graph also distinguishes sleep duration, the process being measured and
measurement itself. Sleep onset produces the asleep state; awakening from
sleep produces the waking state. There are 7 new non-genus relations.
