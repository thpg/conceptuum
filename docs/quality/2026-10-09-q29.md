## Corrected structure and vocabulary

Hunting 1905 already has many animal capability assertions. It now belongs
to physical action instead of necessarily human social action. Human fishing
1910 receives its own physical-action genus. Their blanket mutual exclusion
is rejected. All inherited animal capabilities and the explicit negation
for non-predatory organisms are preserved.

The U1 shared-descendant scan falls from seven to **6 exclusion conflicts**,
with no new conflicting pair or witness. It covers accepted nonzero genus
paths and codes 61, 63 and 64, not all possible semantic contradictions.

Existing angling 13131 receives the standard Russian label `ужение рыбы`,
real English terms and a fishing genus. The method is separated from
recreational purpose. The fishing rod now belongs to fishing gear and has
angling as its more specific purpose. Generic net 1606 loses its universal
fishing purpose; the new fishing-net subtype carries that purpose instead.

Fisher 5303 becomes a person role that can be recreational or occupational.
Only the commercial subtype necessarily belongs to worker. Two adjectives
previously classified as natural objects become properties. The generated
form `рыболовецкее` is removed; the standard adjective is preserved.

The duplicate fishing record 16916 had only one positive genus edge and no
children, facts, explicit negations or rejected history. Its concept row,
three term rows and genus edge 21000 are archived in the manifest. The
attested rare noun `рыбачение` moves to fishing 1910. The malformed extra-vowel
form `рыбачиение` and English-tagged Russian verb are discarded; correctly
tagged `рыбачить` is supplied. Unfamiliarity is not evidence that a word does
not exist. The old `удение` label is corrected for the modern angling sense
without declaring every historical use of that spelling invalid.

## New coverage

Fishing purpose, method and location are separate axes. Recreational angling
and recreational shore fishing have two genera. Commercial and household-food
fishing remain distinct restricted purposes without a universal exclusion.
Ice fishing does not imply angling, recreation or safe ice conditions.

Gear includes lines, handlines, hooks, reels, floats, sinkers, artificial
lures, artificial flies and several net types. A fishhook need not be barbed.
The explicitly reel-equipped rod has a reel component; other rods do not
inherit that requirement. Artificial flies are artifacts, and angler means
a person here, not an anglerfish. Participant capabilities and equipment
purposes connect the new families to activities.

## Sources and dictionary corrections

The pinned [Open English WordNet 2025 base edition](https://en-word.net/downloads)
and [Q11 source notices](2026-10-09-q11.md#sources-and-reuse) remain in use.
Archive SHA-256:
`9ca6d1dcb75f822fdd66617f7d9da48142ace38dd544d6ad5e2feca1674ad3fe`.

The [FAO fishing-gear identification guide](https://www.fao.org/4/y4411e/y4411e0i.htm)
and [gear-classification overview](https://www.fao.org/responsible-fishing/background/classification-of-fishing-gears/en/)
support equipment and method distinctions. The dictionary's restrictive
material, component, depth and hauling descriptions are not copied as
universal properties. [NOAA's fishing categories](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/insight/understanding-fisheries-management-united-states)
support purpose distinctions; its national management rules are not imported.
[FWS ice-fishing coverage](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/lake-ilo/visit-us/activities/ice-fishing)
supports the setting label without transferring local rules.

[NOAA's barbless-hook description](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/pacific-islands/resources-fishing/barbless-circle-hooks)
provides a counterexample to the dictionary's mandatory-barb fishhook gloss.
[Cambridge's fisher entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/fisherman)
supports the broader person role, and its [fishing entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/fishing)
supports equipment and activity wording.

Gramota's [fishing verb](https://gramota.ru/meta/rybachit),
[angling noun](https://gramota.ru/meta/uzhenie),
[fishing adjective](https://gramota.ru/meta/rybolovnyy) and
[fishery adjective](https://gramota.ru/meta/rybolovetskiy) entries support
Russian sense and spelling choices. The [dictionary entry for the rare fishing noun](https://slovar.cc/rus/tolk/102694.html)
supports retaining it as an alias rather than deleting it as a supposed nonword.
